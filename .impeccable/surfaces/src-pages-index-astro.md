# Surface: home and case pages

Mode: Experience. Audience: recruiters and design leads skimming for a Senior/Lead UX designer. Action: email Musa. Proof: ATHLIQ real screens, adidas outcome and rebuilt wireframe, Happy Italy and client site comps.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The home page is a kinetic show reel of Musa's work: type that builds itself and stretches as you scroll, project chapters that take over the whole screen, phones that play their real screens. It refuses the static portfolio of a centred intro above a grid of equal mockup cards. The sketch-to-shipped plotting moves to the case pages, where it explains the work. Each case page opens on a cover (masthead, cover star, cover lines) that continues the curtain hand-off.
OWN-WORLD: Dark by default: black #000000 and #131313 grounds, white type, lime #E1FB15 for the primary action, highlights and full-screen panels, green #32D583 as the second panel colour. Light theme: white ground, #131313 ink, lime and green only as panels or fills carrying black type. Russo One for every heading, Tomorrow for everything else. Full-bleed square panels, phones tilted in 3D with a soft offset shadow, a lime cursor that grows into a View label over work. Each chapter and cover carries its name or number at hero scale.
STORY: In seconds: UX designer who designs apps and builds them. Then a statement that lights up word by word, three project chapters (adidas store app, ATHLIQ, Happy Italy), small-business sites, how he works, 2013 to now, about, say hi. A case opens on its cover, then summary and facts, then the story with sketch-to-shipped devices.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Wordmark top-left, Work, About, Contact and the theme toggle top-right. "I design apps / and build them." in Russo One, centred, building itself letter by letter, over an orbit of real work (adaptation chosen by Musa on 3 Nov 2026 after getartcraft.com: "I do not want to focus solely on 1 product, but showcase a lot of things I have done"): about 15 tiles on two counter-rotating rings in CSS 3D (ATHLIQ at most five including two screen-recording loops, the adidas bulk-scan flow only as a redrawn wireframe, Happy Italy, client websites), thin lime, green and white orbit arcs, tiles step aside where the words are, they fly in from depth after the headline builds and the camera dollies through them on scroll. Intro line and the lime Email me button centred under the headline; the ticker runs along the bottom edge. Every home section after the hero opens with a numbered bar (01 Work to 05 Contact) after the same reference: the rule draws in, ticks land on frame lines in the gutter, the bar holds the top through its section, takes a chapter's colours over a colour chapter, and its lime foot fills with progress. Case cover first viewport: the name (adidas: "1.5 min" under a struck "5 to 8 min") fitted to the width in the case colour, the work rising through its lower edge, outcome title bottom-left and three facts bottom-right; one pinned beat stretches the name away while the work centres and plays its move.
FORM: Kinetic show reel, chosen by Musa on 2 Nov 2026 from three proposed directions (kinetic reel, pixels to product, scoreboard); hero orbit and section bars added on 3 Nov 2026 at his request, after getartcraft.com. Case covers: direction A "cover star", chosen by Musa on 3 Nov 2026 from three (cover star, into the screen, problem first). seed: none (unavailable)
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
